# Applicant walkthrough — suggested recording path

Record yourself using the app and explaining the tradeoffs. The assignment requires your own video link; this file is a recording guide, not a substitute.

1. **Purpose (20 seconds).** Open the Command Center. Explain why a number should carry its source, computation and human decision rather than force a reviewer to rebuild context across tabs.
2. **Evidence (45 seconds).** Open the mileage finding. Show D23 page 3, ev-0404 and the 3,700-mile repeated sheet. Contrast the 11,800-mile engine value with distinct 8,100-mile page totals. Explain synthetic source rendering and coordinates.
3. **Human change (45 seconds).** In Working, record verification method/reason and an overlay. Show the original, author, reason and estimated effects. Explain the supplied-rate conversion factor and why the prototype does not claim to rerun the engine. Reload and show persistence; show History and reversible actions.
4. **Scale (30 seconds).** Show source-based sales cohorts, pagination with full totals, asset Federal/CA/AMT treatment and the aligned state split. Explain that packet imports and version counts are dynamic; show Import rather than implying arbitrary-document OCR.
5. **People and control (40 seconds).** Ask a question, show a blocking note, explain content-bound approvals and server revision checks. Switch to the client or examiner and show the reduced data surface. Explain that the persona switcher is fictional, while operation permissions are server-enforced.
6. **After review (30 seconds).** Show missing filing gates, freeze/amendment behavior through the recorded tests, the defense export and carryforward handoff. State that filing/signatures are simulations.
7. **Audit follow-up (40 seconds).** Show the prior-year variance filter and unread-change acknowledgment. In Assets, open Books to tax and Payments; show the tied books bridge and agency differences. Explain direct payment/credit effects and why source corrections do not rerun the engine. In Filing, show notice dossiers and complete archive/carryforward, including the requirement for a genuine next-year packet.
8. **Tradeoffs (30 seconds).** Explain embedded Postgres for local evaluation and the configured Supabase/Postgres adapter. Identify the open public-demo role model, remaining real authentication and operational recovery and observed CPA/new-hire usability. Disclose AI-assisted development and deterministic runtime checks.

Avoid showing full identifiers, private database credentials, or an access token in the video. Demonstrate on the invented packet or disposable data. Include the app URL, core-idea document, error analysis and your own hosted video URL in the final submission.
