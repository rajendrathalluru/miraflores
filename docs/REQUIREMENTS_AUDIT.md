## Prioritized work before claiming completeness

1. **Payment/credit effects:** introduce dedicated 1:1 balance/tax adjustment semantics with jurisdiction and sign. A probe adding $100 to F1040.33 through the currently offered `interest` category yields **+$34.80** Federal rather than the packet README's payment effect of **-$100**. Generic rate/factor inputs do not safely encode payment/credit meaning.
2. **Year-over-year flags:** read `starter_standards.variance_review`, surface material swings and require reasoned variance conclusions where appropriate.
3. **Since-last-visit changes:** consume the stored seen revision to show changed lines, evidence and decisions for the current reviewer, with a clear acknowledgment action. Current full activity history is not this feature.
4. **Remaining independent scenario checks:** implement source-based CA wage/paystub, supplemental stock basis, HVAC review and duplicate-document candidates; validate basis rollforward/earlier incomplete state without falsely flagging already-computed current gain. Detect review concerns without inventing tax law or overwriting sources.
5. **Polymorphic editing:** implement validated extracted-field and asset overrides (including identity correction records) or narrow the documented architecture. Preserve originals and keep downstream estimates explicit.
6. **Firm policy scope:** store standards by firm and apply them consistently to engagements, rather than labeling engagement-local records “firm-wide.”
7. **Carryforward completeness:** define/import a next-year handoff that preserves review records and evidence/history, with a stable prior-engagement link and explicit unknowns. Current subset export does not satisfy “everything.”
8. **Reconciliation and context usability:** dedicated books-to-tax and payment bridges, complete clickable amount coverage, clear prior-preparer comparison, and a usable client/notice record package.
9. **Final acceptance and submission packaging:** repeat the full browser workflow after fixes, verify the exact hosted revision, produce a one-page formatted idea/model handout and record the applicant's own video.
