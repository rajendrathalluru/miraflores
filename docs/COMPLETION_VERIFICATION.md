## Verification scope

The baseline was not rewritten. QA mutations used disposable databases and clearly named synthetic engagements. No review conclusions were automatically applied to the user's shared Supabase engagement.

Regression tests cover domain rules, independent checks, direct tax effects, source/asset edits, masking, graph resolution, bulk undo, policy unread markers, complete archive integrity and printable-report escaping. API suites exercise SQL transactions, shared policy, concurrency, roles, original files, archives, reports and lifecycle gates. Current command/results are recorded in the hand-in checklist after the final run.

The browser walkthrough observed source-backed reconciliation saving, exact payment effects, asset-source correction, notice-response/download controls, complete archive/import controls, a fully gated synthetic filing freeze, an amended cycle above its preserved frozen record, disabled examiner mutations, and the scoped client portal. A browser-native amendment prompt was replaced with an in-page, reasoned form. This is not an observation of real CPA/new-hire usability.

## Deliberate limits that remain

- The runtime does not rerun a tax engine or OCR documents. The packet explicitly supplies the computations/rates/alternatives. Corrections to source evidence do not silently rewrite dependent engine lines. Human conclusions and supported return-line overlays remain separate actions.
- The runner identifies review concerns. It does not automatically decide a tax treatment, substantiate unknown basis, extract the unread broker lot, or replace a professional conclusion. The unread lot can be documented against its printed page and reflected through a supported return-line adjustment; no fictitious extracted pipeline record is created.
- What-if comparisons use the five supplied alternatives. Arbitrary combinations or automatic replacement of the engine's complete asset schedule are not claimed.
- The complete archive is private original data. Public role switching is intentionally a demonstration, not genuine staff authentication or tenant membership. Real client use still needs identity, tenant access policies and deployment operations.
- A next-year engagement requires a genuine next-year packet. The app does not invent a future return. Imported historic approvals are retained in the archive but do not become fresh identity proof.
- Vercel's body limits can reject larger evidence or complete archives. Use a suitably configured container host for those sizes, or implement private object-storage transfers before claiming larger serverless uploads.
- Docker's engine was unavailable during the earlier image check; the Dockerfile and standalone output are prepared, but a real Docker build/run remains an operational check.
- No applicant video has been recorded. The applicant must record and supply their own video link, and observe the promised CPA/new-hire usability bar.
- The changed code must still be committed/pushed and the public deployment redeployed. Local and disposable production tests do not update the hosted URL.
