# Errors found and how the design exposes them

The fictional packet deliberately contains conflicting evidence. This report distinguishes demonstrated numerical discrepancies from judgments that still need evidence and authority. Source references are the packet's stable IDs. No figures below establish a final corrected return: the supplied engine cannot be rerun, and independent what-if results cannot simply be added together.

These sixteen curated queue items are useful starting points, rather than proof that every area is complete. The detailed records and completeness review also need to cover basis gaps, corrected K-1s, education/dependent facts, foreign/outside filings, depreciation differences, passive losses and carryovers. All documented conclusions should keep their authority, evidence and unresolved assumptions.

## Why this design helps

The source viewer preserves what was printed even when the pipeline read something else, and an unextracted page remains visible. Calculation and reverse-destination links show the reach of a mistake. Verification is a named person's documented method, never an extraction-confidence badge. Proposed adjustments retain the engine value and reason; reversals preserve history. Later evidence can reopen a conclusion and invalidate approvals. Those distinctions make both confident mistakes and weak substantiation visible without manufacturing a final computed return.
