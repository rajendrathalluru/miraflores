# Miraflores: a return made of reviewable claims

**Core idea.** A workpaper should let a reviewer answer four questions about any claim: What is the evidence? How did it affect the return? What judgment changed it? Who stands behind it? The interface starts with open work and follows links into documents, calculations and decisions. It is a working evidence record, rather than a recreation of a spreadsheet.

**Three distinct layers.** Printed source content, extracted readings and engine results remain separate. A 97% confidence reading can still be wrong. The original packet is immutable; every accepted adjustment keeps the original, proposed treatment, reason, author and estimated effect. Simulated documents identify their document, page and field. Text excerpts are approximate because the supplied coordinates are synthetic and there are no original page images.

**Data model.** The supplied packet contains `Document → Page → ExtractedValue` and `ReturnVersion → ReturnLine`. Each line links to input lines, rule references and source references, forming a calculation graph; documents can be followed forward into their destinations. Stable evidence references also reach client messages, prior-year records, assets, agency records and related returns. A separate revisioned `Workspace` contains adjustments, notes, verification records, judgments, questions/answers, completeness dispositions, assignments, time and other workflow records. Audit events retain author, time and target. Approvals bind to a content revision. Filing snapshots preserve a copy and content hash; amendments are new cycles linked to their frozen parent.

**Architecture.** A Next.js modular monolith keeps browser contracts apart from server packet access, the command reducer, persistence and optional investigation tools. Commands validate roles, references and expected revision on the server. A SQLite compare-and-swap commits a change, its audit and any approval invalidation together. Private uploaded bytes stay outside public assets. SQLite and local files make this prototype easy to run; genuine authentication, firm isolation, PostgreSQL/private object storage and deployment operations are separate production integrations.

**Review and trust.** Verification records state the method and supporting conclusion. Completeness covers every document and tax area, including unused evidence and outside filings. Open review notes and unmet review gates block freezing. Independent reviewers cannot approve work they prepared. Read models expose the supplied detailed schedules, prior-year comparisons and five exact precomputed what-ifs without pretending to rerun the engine. Curated adjustment effects use supplied sensitivity rates; general line overlays retain explicitly user-entered estimates.

**AI and tradeoffs.** Assistance is optional and read-only: guided checks work without a key; live AI can investigate masked sources with bounded tools. A person still records the decision. The prototype prioritizes traceability and durable review over automatic filing or a new tax engine. Agency events and authorizations are simulations, and the applicant must still supply a public deployment and recorded walkthrough for submission.
