## Important correction to the design example

D09 and D10 are duplicate payroll uploads, but the delivered wage-source lists use D08 and D09, not D10, in **v1, v2 and v3**. It would be wrong to claim these versions double-counted the wages or to subtract that W-2 again. The app surfaces the duplicate as an informational completeness/disposition concern unless both readings actually feed wages.

The architectural example names D30 for the broker mismatch; current v3 uses corrected D31. Findings follow the selected version rather than a hardcoded document/error list.

## Inherited diagnostics

The engine's missing crypto basis, incomplete earlier-year basis, late K-1, education-credit phaseout, California AMT, outside FBAR/1099, due-diligence and questionnaire warnings remain labeled as inherited diagnostics. Displaying one is not claimed as an independent discovery.

Tax treatments and filing remain professional review decisions. The prototype preserves evidence and exposes disagreements; a passing software test is not a completed household tax review.
